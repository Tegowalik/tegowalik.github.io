# Content roadmap

Ideas for future Tegowalik website sections. These are intentionally not exposed as empty pages on the live site.

## Statistics

- Import the Excel sheets from previous layouts.
- Compare track inventories and usage across builds.
- Include track types, curve radii, switches, total rail length, trains, motors, sensors, and LEGO elements used for bridges.
- Distinguish between pieces owned and pieces used in a specific layout.

## Building tips

- Bridge supports for TrixBrix switches
- Suspension bridge
- 20-way crossing
- Train elevator
- Randomized switch routing

## Train gallery

- One entry for every train in the collection.
- Use a photo of each train running on one of the Tegowalik layouts where possible.
- Include model name, manufacturer or source, motor configuration, length, and links to relevant videos.
